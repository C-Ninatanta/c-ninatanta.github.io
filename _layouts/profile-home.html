About Me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering Ph.D. researcher at Washington State University, working with Prof. Ming Luo in the Mechanically-Intelligent Autonomous Robotics (MIAR) Laboratory.

My research spans mechanical design, rapid prototyping, soft robotics, manipulation, and experimental evaluation. I care about turning rigorous research into machines that can do useful work.
