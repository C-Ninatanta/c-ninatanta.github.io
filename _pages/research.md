<div class="pf-article">
  <h2>Robotics for the real world</h2>
  <p>My work centers on a simple question: how can we make robots more capable in the unpredictable environments where people actually need them? I approach this through the design of soft robotic hardware, physically grounded modeling, experimental testing, and human–robot interaction.</p>
  <p>At Washington State University's Mechanically-Intelligent Autonomous Robotics (MIAR) Laboratory, I work on robotic systems with applications in agriculture and human-centered interaction, including lightweight soft grippers, growing manipulators, and haptic interfaces for teleoperation.</p>
</div>
