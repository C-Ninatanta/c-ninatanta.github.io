01 / The researcher

Researching the space between robot design and meaningful interaction.

Chris NinatantaWashington State University · MIAR Laboratory

I'm a mechanical engineering Ph.D. student at Washington State University, advised by Prof. Ming Luo in the Mechanically-Intelligent Autonomous Robotics (MIAR) Laboratory.

My work combines mechanical design, prototypes, robot modeling, and experimental validation. I'm especially interested in robots that operate in unstructured environments and connect human insight with robotic capability.
